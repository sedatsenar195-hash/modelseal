        How it works

        Three steps, one permanent record.

        01

        Author

        Seal

        The author hashes each model file locally (SHA-256) and publishes a small manifest to Arweave, where it’s permanent and can’t be edited or deleted.

        02

        Author

        Share

        The author lists the manifest’s transaction ID with the model.

        03

        Anyone

        Verify

        Anyone hashes their download locally and compares it to the permanent record: Match, Mismatch or Unknown.

        Limitations

        A fingerprint proves a file hasn’t changed since it was sealed. It doesn’t prove the original model was safe, or that the sealer is the real author. Author wallet signatures are on the roadmap.
